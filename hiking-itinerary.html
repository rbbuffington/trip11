AllTrails Link ↗

⚠ Daily mileage breakdown TBD
⚠ Night 1 campsite location TBD
Key Rules
• No campfires anywhere in Shining Rock Wilderness — stove only
• No blazes — download offline map before leaving cell service (Gaia GPS or AllTrails offline)
• Bear canisters required in Pisgah Ranger District
• Camp at least 1,000 ft from any trail
⚠ Trail has real storm damage from Tropical Storm Fred (2021) — one sketchy section on Shining Creek Trail ~1.5 miles from road. Passable but scrambly. Recent reports say conditions haven't worsened since Helene.
